Shipped the Understand quiz. Before the Deliver gate opens, the run's owner answers 1–5 multiple-choice questions, plus one per risk trigger touched. A new read-only `examiner` role writes them, and they're shown as pop-ups. The engine grades each pick against the diff. After a wrong pick it explains the point and asks again with fresh choices, until every question is understood. No size or unattended run skips it. The questions go into the PR body, and `metrics.understanding` is recorded in state and the retro.
